**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** All Hospital Staff
**FROM:** Human Resources Department
**DATE:** October 23, 2023
**SUBJECT:** Mandatory Updates to Annual Leave Booking Procedures

To ensure consistent operational coverage and resource allocation across all departments, we are updating our annual leave booking procedures, effective November 15th.

Moving forward, all leave requests must be submitted through the updated HR Portal. Please note that staff are now required to submit requests a minimum of **six (6) weeks** in advance.

Managers will review all requests based on departmental scheduling requirements and anticipated staffing levels. Requests submitted late, or those that significantly impact essential services, may be deferred or denied to ensure patient care continuity.

We appreciate your cooperation in adopting these new guidelines. For detailed instructions or access to the portal, please visit the HR Intranet site or contact your direct supervisor.

Thank you for your commitment to maintaining our high standard of care.
