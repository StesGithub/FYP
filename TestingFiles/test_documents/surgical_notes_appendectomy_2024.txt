**Pre-Op Diagnosis:** Acute Appendicitis

*   **Surgeon:** Dr. Evelyn Reed, MD

**Procedure Detail:**
The patient underwent a standard laparoscopic appendectomy. Following general anaesthesia and optimal positioning, three small trocar incisions were made. Pneumoperitoneum was established and maintained. Upon entering the peritoneal cavity, the inflamed appendix was easily identified. Careful dissection was performed using electrocautery to isolate the appendiceal base. The mesoappendix was ligated and divided, and the appendix was completely removed. Examination of the specimen confirmed acute appendicitis, with minor associated localized pericecal inflammation. Hemostasis was confirmed throughout the procedure. The instruments were removed, and the fascia layers were closed. Estimated blood loss was minimal (<25ml).

**Post-Op Instructions:**
Patient tolerated the procedure well. To be managed with IV antibiotics and continuous pain management. Advised early ambulation and diet advancement as tolerated. Follow-up required in 7 days. Incisions to be monitored for signs of infection.
