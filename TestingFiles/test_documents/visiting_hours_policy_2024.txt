## **Hospital Visitor Policy: Ensuring Rest and Recovery**

To ensure a healing environment for all patients, we maintain designated visiting hours. These guidelines allow our staff to provide continuous care while promoting the necessary rest required for recovery.

**Standard Visiting Hours:**
*   **General Wards:** [Insert Day] – [Insert Day], 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM
*   **Intensive Care Units (ICU):** [Insert Day] – [Insert Day], 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (by prior staff approval)

**Important Guidelines:**
*   Please respect 'Quiet Hours' (e.g., 10:00 PM – 7:0:0 AM).
*   Keep visits brief and supportive.
*   Visitors must be able to provide valid identification and must be accompanied by a registered escort if required by department staff.

**Emergency Visits:**
Should you have an urgent need to visit outside of posted hours, please check with the nursing station. In medical emergencies, access is always prioritized.
