# Understanding Your Rights as a Patient

**Your well-being and dignity are paramount.** At [Hospital Name], we are committed to ensuring you are respected, safe, and fully informed throughout your care journey. Understanding your rights empowers you to be an active participant in your health decisions.

**You have the right to:**

*   **Privacy:** Confidentiality regarding your medical information is strictly maintained.
*   **Respect:** To be treated with dignity and courtesy by all staff.
*   **Information:** To receive clear, understandable explanations about your diagnosis, treatment options, and prognosis.
*   **Consent:** To be informed and given the opportunity to agree (or refuse) any treatment *after* understanding the risks and benefits.
*   **Participation:** To speak up, ask questions, and have your concerns addressed promptly.

**If you feel uncomfortable, unheard, or concerned about your care, please speak up. You can ask to speak with a Nurse Manager, Patient Advocate, or utilize our confidential Feedback Desk.**

*Your voice matters.*
