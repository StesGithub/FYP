**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** All Department Staff, Clinical and Support Services
**FROM:** Infection Prevention & Control Committee
**DATE:** October 26, 2023
**SUBJECT:** MANDATORY UPDATES TO HOSPITAL-WIDE INFECTION CONTROL PROTOCOLS

Please be advised of mandatory revisions to our facility’s Infection Control Protocol, effective immediately. These updates reflect best practices in healthcare safety and are crucial for maintaining our safe environment.

Staff are required to review the updated guidelines regarding Standard and Transmission-Based Precautions. Key adjustments include:

1.  **Hand Hygiene:** Strict adherence to the "Five Moments for Hand Hygiene" remains paramount. Use alcohol-based sanitizer or soap/water upon entry/exit of patient care areas.
2.  **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Revised donning and doffing procedures for gowns and masks have been implemented. All staff must participate in the mandatory refresh training session.
3.  **Equipment Handling:** Enhanced protocols for the cleaning and transport of medical devices and linens are now in effect.

Compliance with these updated procedures is non-negotiable and integral to patient and staff safety. Managers will reinforce these guidelines during shift briefings. Please direct all immediate questions to the Infection Control Department.
